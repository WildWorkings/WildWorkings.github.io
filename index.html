Wild Workings Uploader

How nature actually works

Wild Workings is an educational YouTube channel that explains the science behind the natural world: animals, plants, weather, oceans and the planet itself.

Wild Workings Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own videos to the channel owner's own YouTube account and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time through the YouTube Data API.

The tool has no other users, does not access anyone else's account, and does not collect or share personal data.
